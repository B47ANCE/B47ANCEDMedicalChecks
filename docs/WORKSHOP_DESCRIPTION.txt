[h1]B47ANCE'd Medical Checks[/h1]

Take control of who can examine your character's medical condition in Project Zomboid multiplayer.

B47ANCE'd Medical Checks adds examination approval requests, allowing you to decide who can view your character's medical information.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[b]Two Permission Modes[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Always Ask:[/b] Approve or decline every examination request.
[*][b]Only Ask Once Per Player:[/b] Approve someone once, and they'll be remembered for future examinations.
[/list]

Declined requests are never remembered as approvals.

[b]Manage Permissions[/b]

View your previously approved examiners and revoke individual permissions at any time through Medical Checks settings.

Permissions are tied to player accounts, not character names, and are managed by the server.

[h2]Server Settings[/h2]

Server administrators can choose between four examination policies:

[list]
[*][b]Player Choice:[/b] Players select their preferred permission mode.
[*][b]Force Always Ask:[/b] Every examination requires approval.
[*][b]Force Only Ask Once:[/b] Remember approved examiners.
[*][b]No Examination Requests:[/b] Disable examination requests.
[/list]

Additional settings control default preferences, approval retention, request cooldowns, response timeouts, and diagnostic logging.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Project Zomboid Build 42.21
[*]Mod ID: B47ANCEDMedicalChecks
[*]Lua-only, no dependencies
[*]Install on both server and clients
[*]Preserves vanilla First Aid, treatments, and examination mechanics
[/list]

[h2]Development Status[/h2]

[b]⚠ Multiplayer Testing Build[/b]

Dedicated-server multiplayer, player-hosted multiplayer, and in-game UI rendering have not yet been tested.

Offline Python/Lupa tests do not guarantee in-game functionality.

[b]Known Limitations:[/b] Revoking permission does not close an already-open health panel. Switching approval retention back to Persistent may restore previously stored approvals.

Back up server configuration and approval files before testing.

[h2]Health Care Compatibility[/h2]
Medical Checks remains a standalone mod. Its features are planned for inclusion in Health Care (Mod ID: B47ANCEDHealthCare; Workshop ID: 3788592396).

When Health Care is enabled, Medical Checks leaves its own features inactive to avoid duplicate behavior. Existing Medical Checks preferences and approvals are preserved. Fully restart the game/server after changing the enabled mod set.
